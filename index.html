About

Research interests at the intersection of Digital Humanities, Library and Information Science, and cultural heritage, with a focus on knowledge representation, semantic interoperability, and digital memory infrastructures.

Current research addresses Romanian documentary and intangible heritage, historical print culture, cultural data modelling, and knowledge organisation.
