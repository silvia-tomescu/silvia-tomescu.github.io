About

My research lies at the intersection of Digital Humanities, Library and Information Science, and cultural heritage, focusing on knowledge representation, digital memory infrastructures, and semantic interoperability.

Particular areas of interest include Romanian documentary and intangible heritage, early modern print culture, TEI-based modelling of historical sources, and the modelling of cultural knowledge.
