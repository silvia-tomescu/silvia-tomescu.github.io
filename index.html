About

Digital humanities researcher focused on cultural heritage ontologies and knowledge graphs, with expertise in semantic modelling using RDF, OWL, CIDOC CRM, and LRMoo. Emphasis on the structuring, enrichment, and interoperability of cultural and research data.

Contributions to international conferences and initiatives in digital humanities, digital cultural heritage, and open science. Active peer reviewer for international journals and conferences, and research evaluator involved in the assessment of research proposals within national funding frameworks (UEFISCDI).
